A few things you might be wondering

Good questions.

Why don’t I need to create an account?

Your phone number is how Rosa recognizes you. You request a password part by text, and Rosa sends a link back to that number. There’s no separate Rosa login or username.

How should I choose my two password parts?

Use a different, hard-to-guess part for every website. A longer phrase can be easier to type from your phone than random characters. Choose a private part you can remember, and combine the two to meet each website’s password requirements. Don’t use the examples on this page as real passwords.

Keep your private part to yourself. A shared private part is not a guarantee of security: if a complete password is exposed on one website, that part could be exposed too.

Is SMS secure? What if someone gets my phone?

SMS has risks, including SIM swapping, interception, and recycled phone numbers. Someone with access to your texts may also be able to open Rosa’s links and retrieve the parts you stored.

Keeping a private part out of Rosa means those stored parts alone aren’t your complete passwords. It doesn’t eliminate every risk. Protect your phone and mobile carrier account, and use stronger authentication on websites that offer it.

Rosa aims to make better password habits easy. It does not promise the strongest possible protection, and SMS is not end-to-end encrypted.

What if my phone number changes?

Arrange to transfer your saved password parts before you lose access to your old number. Do this while you can still receive texts at that number.

What if I forget my private part?

Rosa can only return the part you saved with it. It cannot recover the private part you kept in your head. If you forget that part, you’ll need to use the affected website’s password reset process.

Will Rosa send me unwanted texts?

Rosa responds to your requests and sends service messages, such as information about payment when your free retrievals are nearly used up.

Can I remove my stored passwords?

Yes. You can remove the password data you’ve saved with Rosa. Text Rosa for help.
